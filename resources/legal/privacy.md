# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 18 November 2026 · Draft pending legal review_

This policy explains what **[OPERATOR LEGAL NAME]** ("Landpot", "we") collects when you use landpot.fun and the Landpot game, why, and what you can do about it. We collect as little as the game needs. There are no ads, no analytics trackers, and no email sign-up.

## What we collect

**Your session.** When you open the site we create an anonymous player record: a random identifier, a short public handle, and the date it was created. A sign-in cookie (`lp_refresh`) keeps you on the same record. We store only a one-way hash of that cookie's value.

**Your wallet address, if you sign in with a wallet.** We record the address that signed the sign-in message. We never see your private keys. The signature proves the address is yours. It does not let us move funds.

**Your matches.** Each match is recorded: the map and settings, each player's chosen name and handle, every in-game action, and the result. This is how the game works (it replays inputs) and how paid matches are settled and disputes checked.

**Network data at join.** When you join a match, your IP address and a Cloudflare Turnstile token are checked to stop bots. We use them for that check and do not store them in our database. Server logs, which can include IP addresses, are kept for up to 30 days for security.

**On your device.** The game stores your settings, chosen name, and similar preferences in your browser's local storage. They stay on your device.

## What is public

Your username and in-game actions are visible to other players during a match. Finished matches are published as replays with player identifiers removed. Your wallet address is not shown in replays. Once paid matches launch, entries and payouts are blockchain transactions: they are public and permanent by the nature of the blockchain, and are linked to your address, not to us.

## Who else processes data

- **Cloudflare** provides Turnstile bot checks and may serve parts of the site. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.
- **Your wallet provider** handles the signing request on your device under its own policy.
- **Our hosting provider** stores the servers and database.

We do not sell personal data or share it for advertising.

## How long we keep it

- Player records and wallet links: until you ask us to delete them.
- Sign-in sessions: 30 days after last use.
- Match records: kept, because they are needed to verify results and settle paid matches. When a player record is deleted, its matches stay but are no longer linked to anyone.
- Security logs: up to 30 days.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you can ask to see, correct, export, or delete your data, and you can object to how we use it. Write to us at the address below with your public handle or wallet address. To delete your session from this device, sign out or clear the site's cookies and storage. We cannot delete blockchain transactions; no one can.

Where the GDPR applies, we process your data to provide the game you asked for (contract) and to keep it fair and secure (legitimate interests).

## Children

The Service is not directed at children under 13, and paid matches are for adults only. If you believe a child under 13 has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.
